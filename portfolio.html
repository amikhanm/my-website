About

My first commission was paid in lunch money. I was ten, and my classmates were my clients. At eight, I had a painting exhibited at Doha's old international airport through the Al Koora Art Project.

As a tetrachromat, I see colour differently, and that became the centre of my practice. I work across drawing, painting, and mixed media, studying what colours mean and bringing that into everything I make.

My portfolio has been positively reviewed by Rhode Island School of Design, and I hold offers to study Fine Art at UAL Chelsea College of Arts and Central Saint Martins. I'm on a gap year building my practice through short courses before taking up my deferred place at UAL.

Alongside my fine art, I design branding, invitations, posters, social graphics, and print materials for individuals and businesses, from a cosy brand for a dog-walking company to an intricate wedding stationery suite.

Tools: Adobe Suite, Procreate, Krita, Figma, Webflow, DaVinci Resolve.
Languages: English, Filipino, Kapampangan, Spanish, Portuguese.

How I see colour

I'm a tetrachromat, which means I see colour differently. That became the centre of my practice. I study the significance of colours, how they're read across cultures and contexts, and bring that into every piece I make.
